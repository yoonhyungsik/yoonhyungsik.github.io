About

My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning, computer vision, and computational molecular science. I am particularly interested in learning representations and dynamics directly from scientific observations.

I am also interested in using feedback from vision-language models to guide intermediate steps in diffusion and flow-based image generation. Research statement
